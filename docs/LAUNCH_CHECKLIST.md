# Document Studio — launch messaging & positioning

**Release:** experimental `v0.1.0-alpha.6`. Do not label this as a stable v1 or a model-independent enterprise platform.

## The one-sentence story

> **AI models change. Your company's document standards shouldn't.**

Document Studio is a review-first, open-source toolkit for turning authorized document references into **approved, reusable company document conventions** and a tailored ChatGPT document plugin. The value is not a slide template or a clever prompt: it is **keeping organizational writing, design and review standards available as AI tools and teams evolve**.

### Three communication pillars

1. **Separate the standard from the model.** The model and its version may change; the Company Pack is independently reviewable and versioned.
2. **Give organizations control.** Evidence, human rule approval, source rights and explicit QA status are part of the workflow.
3. **Consistency without creative stagnation.** Approved grammar constrains the essentials; new reports may use genuinely different layouts, imagery and page composition.

### Be precise

- **Implemented:** authorized PDF/PPTX evidence extraction; draft Company Pack review and approval; fictional/public-authorized plugin ZIP export; selected language skills; experimental Layout Grammar integration.
- **Not verified or shipped:** uniform results across different AI models/versions; multi-model runtime integration; one-click ChatGPT Business installation; private-source internal-only exporter; fully automatic and visually certified HTML/PPTX production.
- The aim is reproducible **standards**, not pixel-identical documents or suppression of human judgment. New model deployments must be compatibility- and quality-checked.

## Launch announcement — English

> **AI models change. Your company's document standards shouldn't.**
>
> Every model upgrade makes AI more capable — but it can also change the tone, structure and design of the documents your team produces. I think teams need a reusable layer of *company standards*, not another magic prompt.
>
> That's why I'm open-sourcing **Document Studio**: a review-first toolkit that analyzes authorized PDFs and presentations, proposes evidence-backed writing/design rules, lets a human approve a **Company Pack**, and packages an organization-aware document plugin for ChatGPT.
>
> The idea: **your documents → your approved rules → your team's document AI.**
>
> It's an experimental alpha, not a one-click enterprise installer. Public-authorized examples and plugin ZIP export work today; private-source internal distribution and visually certified end-to-end generation remain future work. Different models are **not** guaranteed to produce identical results.
>
> Code and fictional demo: https://github.com/wlehd00000-cell/document-studio
>
> If your team struggles with inconsistent AI-generated reports and presentations, I'd welcome feedback or an issue on GitHub.

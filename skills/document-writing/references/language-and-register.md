# Final-language register and equivalence

Use the document's accepted audience, knowledge, purpose, relationship, reading context, disclosure and style examples. Intake choices live in the orchestrator brief. This file owns their effect on words, not another questionnaire.

Write in the actual final language from the first excerpt. Korean headquarters and a Japan branch do not determine reader language or tone. Resolve "we", "head office", 本社・当社・弊社 against the actual organization and speaker. A business Japanese report may use a factual 常体 or polite 敬体 according to its template; never impose one on every Japanese file. Formal Korean may retain sufficient explanation and nominal table labels. For source-backed Korean DEMO report voice, apply [DEMO ESG writing](demo-esg-writing.md) and [connected long-form Korean](demo-esg-korean-longform.md); do not compress the body into title-like noun chains. [Company profiles](demo-esg-writing.md#회사별-프로필과-언어별-적용) distinguish responsibility, technical explanation and field-process emphasis.

Shared terminology means one existing glossary where useful: source term, accepted target term, entity/scope and explicit approval if present. Preserve official names and branded spelling. Do not translate a branch into a different company or standardize ambiguous departments without evidence. Keep raw IDs, URLs and official product names intact.

Naturalness comes from meaning and relationships: who does what, what qualifies it, how the sentences connect and what the reader needs. Repair Japanese subject/predicate mismatches, long modifiers, nested nominal phrases and unclear referents. Translate Korean 진행/공유/대응 by the actual action. Repair Korean mechanical nominal chains, misplaced agents and unnecessary passive forms. Do not add an emotion, anecdote, ranking or causal claim to make a sentence vivid.

Use separate files, paired text or a main file plus translated summary only as selected. Share claim/section IDs and retain facts, dates, units, actors, exclusions and proposal/approval state in each version. A shorter summary is labelled; shorter wording does not imply reduced obligations or stronger certainty. Necessary recipient-specific exclusions follow the accepted disclosure scope while retaining qualifications.

Source equivalence is more than matching digits: a percentage needs its denominator; a date needs its event and time context; a promise needs its actor and condition. Currency, tax and time-zone conversion need an actual basis. Distinguish 検討中・予定・見込み・提案・承認待ち・決定 and Korean equivalents. Keep qualifications close rather than burying them to fit.

If design compresses labels or breaks lines, compare the revised visible wording again and review actual target-language text. Korean word flow and Japanese kinsoku belong to the rendered design review. Natural prose and zero overflow do not prove each other.

# Page families and composition options

The [canonical design system](../system/design-system.md) separates document purpose profiles from page families. Select the real reader question before a composition; do not rotate patterns by quota. The [DEMO casebook](demo-esg-casebook.json) provides observed corporate/editorial spatial relationships, with explicit adaptations.

| Family | Useful composition | Preserve |
|---|---|---|
| cover | Full-bleed photo/concept, product hero, location crop, type-led or restrained institutional cover | Dominant subject, quiet title zone, editable purpose/date, truthful source/concept status |
| chapter_opener | Large image or motif with strong title and restrained section guide | Subject change and useful onward reading; separate from body density |
| editorial | Large scene/object + narrow explanation rail, image + quiet title zone | Focal point and near caption/conditions |
| narrative | Connected paragraphs with local table/chart/photo | Claim-evidence proximity and final-language flow |
| evidence | Original photo/table + exact annotation/detail | Identity, scope, observation and source |
| comparison | Common criteria/table/axes, aligned images/small multiples | Equal criteria/units/scales and local tradeoffs |
| process | Flow, branches, owner lanes, actual authority | Connector meaning and completion condition |
| execution | Shared schedule, owners, prerequisites, exceptions | Confirmed vs proposed commitments |
| appendix | Factbook/reference/data table | Full definitions, periods, group headers and missing-value semantics |

These are roles, not a closed template menu. A meaningful storyboard, annotated plan, chart with explanation, editorial spread or image mosaic may combine roles. Open aligned areas and local rules often express the content better than repeated cards.

[Existing body samples](../assets/demo-esg-design/index.html) preserve the observed reading-report relationships. [New family studies](../assets/demo-esg-design/family-studies.html) show a full-image cover, photo opener, detailed body and actual live-viewing composition, in Korean/Japanese. They contain fictional demonstration content and original conceptual media. Never copy sample values or count as a brief. A full-image cover alone does not certify the body or exported PPTX.

Use [image art direction](demo-esg-image-layout.md) for placement and [visual direction](visual-direction.md) for evidence/encoding. Current actual data, final-language text and accepted template determine the final page.

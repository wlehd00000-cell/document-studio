# Body composition — independent synthetic patterns

The bundled galleries were written for three fictional exercises and contain no observed corporate pages.

- **Connected:** show a four-stage process and its handoff relationships in a single editable view.
- **Detail:** pair one large explanatory visual with a close-up and annotated condition.
- **Essay:** interleave a short narrative, supporting evidence and a decision request.
- **Mosaic:** combine deliberately varied but semantically connected evidence zones.

Examples use the 18→24-minute, 87→93-percent and 42→56-percent *synthetic* metrics. None are results from an employer or publication. Use real source facts and rights-cleared images when creating an actual deliverable.

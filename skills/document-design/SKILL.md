# HTML document design

In the explicitly opted-in [Editorial Consulting Mode](../report-html-pptx/references/editorial-consulting-mode.md), an **early representative HTML preview** is a bounded comparison of substantive visual directions only. Keep it under `planning/previews/`; use real supported copy or conspicuously labelled illustrative content and inspect the rendered sample when possible. It is not canonical `html/index.html`, not final art direction for all pages, and never a verified production Design/Export receipt. After the author selects the direction, return to deep research/writing; resume this normal HTML design workflow only from the accepted final-language manuscript, with freedom to revise the exploratory geometry to fit the evidence and body copy.

For new full work, the [accepted guide baseline](../report-html-pptx/references/accepted-composition-default.md) is the routine composition level: expressive/rich with calm readable detailed bodies. Inspect its relevant actual body pages and implement meaningful images, native diagrams and varied reading relationships in the current content. Preserve explicit style/templates; do not treat this as a fixed canvas, page sequence or image quota.

Reuse the current brief, actual final-language copy, page map and assets. Document composition is fixed to HTML under [authoring routes](../report-html-pptx/system/authoring-routes.md); default delivery uses [HTML-to-PPTX](../html-to-pptx/SKILL.md). Templates constrain actual size, identity and accepted layout; they do not trigger native authoring. Routine missing preferences use the orchestrator's working defaults. Local edits preserve the unaffected composition.

Reader question → supported relationship → **choose the visual medium** → art-directed asset when needed → native labels → composition. For covers, hero images, complex objects/3D scenes or disputed code-only results, use [visual-medium selection](references/visual-medium-selection.md) before detailed HTML. HTML is the layout/integration source, not an instruction to draw every subject in CSS/SVG. Prefer actual generated or verified visuals when pictorial fidelity matters; keep exact business text, data and connectors native. A typography-only or purely native diagram remains valid. Use [design system](../report-html-pptx/system/design-system.md) and [tokens](../report-html-pptx/system/design-tokens.json) for purpose, family, font/grid/palette defaults. Numeric values stay in tokens; scripts/build_design_css.py generates the shared CSS. Reading/report and live-viewing density remain distinct even though both produce PPTX. Recompose live content for distance; preserve detailed conditions in its reading companion when needed.
For new arrow-connected process/relationship diagrams, treat each item as a separate card area and center its title and explanatory text within that area, including the first and last items. Keep connectors in separate lanes; use equal item widths for equivalent stages. Follow [arrow diagram alignment](references/arrow-diagram-alignment.md). Preserve explicit accepted-template alignment outside the requested scope.
New Korean text uses bundled Pretendard (the Korean family); Japanese uses bundled Pretendard JP. Mark each bilingual passage's actual language. Preserve accepted template fonts with located provenance; do not replace either language's required font with a generic fallback or treat embedding alone as actual rendering proof.

Select useful actual relationships from [DEMO cases](../report-html-pptx/references/demo-esg-design.md) and [expression](../report-html-pptx/references/design-expression.md). Expand only selected cases and inspect their actual relevant visuals; do not routinely open the full 100KB casebook or 121KB gallery HTML. Reuse accepted selections. Creativity and visual intensity are independent. Official fonts/assets and meaningful detail stay intact; purposeful color, texture, scale and asymmetry can extend the defaults.

For substantive pages apply [body composition](../report-html-pptx/references/body-composition.md); open a relevant [body study](../report-html-pptx/assets/body-compositions/index.html) only after selecting its page job. Compose connected scenes/local labels, dominant/supporting mosaics, cross-column prose/photo flow or whole/detail views according to the actual question. A cover is not body-quality evidence. Do not repeat an image-and-table split, cards or unsupported oversized numbers on every page. A useful prose/table-only page remains valid.

Use actual paragraphs and source assets from the first meaningful page; do not design around lorem ipsum or empty image slots and call it a proof. Record dominant element and reading sequence in the existing map. Keep project-relative files under html/assets, native business text/table cells/labels and meaningful SVG connectors. Author a specific page directly when a generator cannot express the relationship. [DEMO CSS/examples](../report-html-pptx/assets/demo-esg-design/index.html) and [consulting parts](../report-html-pptx/references/consulting-parts.md) are adaptable relationships, not compulsory page sequences.

When the medium choice calls for a scene, source/generate and inspect the actual hero asset before polishing its surrounding CSS; do not replace a needed building, person or material with placeholder geometry that merely passes the grid audit. Before rich image production, give [visual assets](../visual-assets/SKILL.md) a concrete job through [asset requests](references/visual-asset-requests.md): meaning/subjects, image role, final frame/aspect, crop protection, view, quiet overlay zone, caption, reference role and actual/concept status. Important Korean/Japanese labels, quantities and connectors stay native. Research acquires factual originals; generated facilities/activities are concepts. Planned overlay coordinates are confirmed only after inspecting actual output.

Integrate actual returned files and inspect focal subject, crop, source/concept disclosure, label association and series consistency at final size. Mixed-ratio groups use deliberate edges/heights and caption baselines while preserving packshot/poster identity. Use [image placement](../report-html-pptx/references/demo-esg-image-layout.md) and [user image composition](../report-html-pptx/references/user-image-composition.md) only for their relevant relationship.

Apply writing's [semantic line-break decisions](../document-writing/references/semantic-linebreaks.md) to the actual HTML: preserve the copy, add only justified local breaks, and retain natural wrapping within each reading unit. Keep zero extra paragraph gap when the intent is a simple line break. Recalculate text height, adjacent blocks and footer clearance after adding lines. Do not squeeze type or freeze every physical line.

For a new full document, inspect every page at final size and read titles, prose, prompts, table cells, captions and footer boundaries. For a revision, inspect affected pages plus the sequence; global font/style/canvas changes reopen the full affected set. Readable margins alone do not establish Korean clarity or spacing: return language problems to writing. Keep each text-flow candidate's page/block/line and an actual reviewed disposition under [completion quality](../report-html-pptx/references/completion-quality.md).

Review the sequence and all changed/dense/long-language/new-image pages with [visual review](../report-html-pptx/references/demo-esg-review.md) and [text flow](../report-html-pptx/references/text-flow-and-wrapping.md). Repair relationship/geometry before reducing type. Bind final checks through [project state](../report-html-pptx/system/project-state.md); do not build the whole QA graph before a first useful composition. Global fonts/CSS/canvas changes reopen affected coverage. Return reviewed actual HTML, asset/label IDs and scoped findings to export; HTML review does not certify final PPTX appearance.

## Implement varied explanation in actual body pages

Apply [expression and rhythm](../report-html-pptx/references/visual-expression-and-rhythm.md) before settling on each major body composition. Use appropriate photos/annotated images, illustrations, charts, process/relationship diagrams, timelines and substantive prose from the actual question. Follow useful image choices through to real assets; do not leave placeholders or recommendations only. Mark actual dominant form and narrative movement when practical. Review the body sequence separately for table dependence and repeated visual/narrative patterns, including tables disguised as cards. Keep precise comparison tables where they serve the reader and record why a repeated series is justified.

Report page titles and subheads use [nominal heading style](../report-html-pptx/references/report-heading-style.md). Apply writing's actual phrasing, preserve expected/proposed/completed status, and check headings at final size and after export. HTML report markup declares data-heading-style="nominal"; an explicit message/preserve exception applies only to its agreed scope.
